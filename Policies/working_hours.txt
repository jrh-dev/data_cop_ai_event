Standard hours are 37.5 per week. Overtime must be pre-approved.

Out of hours working is any additional time a permanent staff member spends working on a project outside of their contracted working pattern - i.e. for full time staff 9-5:30 Monday - Friday, or for part time staff any time worked outside of their part time working pattern. 

This does not include flexi-time - where you may work hours outside of your normal hours to make up for time off taken within your normal working hours - for situations like attending an appointment, as this is not additional time. 

If you need to add time and the timesheet is now locked, please contact accounts@airelogic.com with details of the missing time.

If you know you are going away, please complete your future days/weeks timesheet before you go away.
