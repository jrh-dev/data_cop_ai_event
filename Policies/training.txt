Employees may request up to 5 paid training days per year for approved courses.

We've introduced a new mandatory training page, that provides all the links you need to stay up-to-date. Completing the mandatory training relevant to your position is essential as it equips you with the knowledge to work effectively and safely.  Our training programme combines videos, presentations, and online resources to cater to different learning styles.

Training Time allows you to use working hours for the purpose of personal development. It can be requested by filling out a Training Time Request Form which can be found on the intranet along with some additional information here.

Your request will be assessed by HR and you will receive a confirmation email to confirm that your request has been approved and recorded. Once your request has been approved you should notify your team and client (if applicable) as soon as reasonably possible so they can plan around the time that you will be unavailable. 

We’re keen to support everyone with their learning and development, so if there is a cost involved with the training or course you would like to attend, please feel able to request this through Training Time and any costs will be assessed by HR.

There is also an option to record Training Time in your Training Centre time tracker so please ensure you are logging the time accurately on the days that you aren’t working and if you are unsure just speak to one of our friendly HR team.
